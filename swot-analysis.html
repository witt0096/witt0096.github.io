My mission this year

As an HBO-ICT student, I want to build a sustainable routine that allows me to make the most of my ability to learn quickly and work independently. By using my strong planning skills, I will create a realistic schedule that balances attending lectures, independent study, rest, and my other responsibilities. My goal is to find a routine that allows me to maintain my energy throughout the week so that I can consistently perform at my best and get the most out of my studies.

Strengths Weaknesses

Indepedent and fast learning: I am able to learn new material quickly and apply it. I am good at working and studying idependently while staying focused.

Planning: I am good at planning my work and holding myself accountable to my planning.

Fear of failure: I find it difficult to not start stressing weeks in advance for an upcoming test.

Maintaining energy: I find it difficult to maintain my energy over longer periods of time when I have to be in busy envirorments and travel to school for many days a week, especially when I've not had enough sleep.

Opportunities Threats

This programme offers the flexibility of studying independently or working in small groups.

The programme gives me the opportunity to develop my skills, broaden my knowledge and discover which areas I'm most interested in.

A potential threat is other people putting me under pressure to preform well because this adds extra stress to me.

My side job may also be a potential threat, as it requires me to spend additional time working and travelling, which could reduce the energy I have available for my studies.

SMART+ Goals

Goal 1:

I will use my ability to plan well to create a realistic study schedule that includes attending lectures on campus for at least 3 days a week, while also scheduling enough time for rest and independent study.

I measure my progress by tracking how many days per week I attend lectures on campus and comparing this to my planned schedule.

By 31 December 2026, I will evaluate my average campus attendance and how well I have been able to balance attending lectures with rest and independent study.

Goal 2:

I will work towards a more consistent sleep schedule by going to bed before 10 PM on weekdays and before 11 PM on weekends, so that I can maintain my energy better over longer periods of time and feel less stressed.

I will track how many days per week I follow my planned bedtime and use this to monitor how consistently I maintain my sleep schedule.

By 31 December 2026, I will evaluate how consistently I have followed my sleep schedule and whether it has helped me maintain my energy. I will also evaluate the impact on my overall well-being and stress levels.
