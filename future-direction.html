Future Direction

I am currently leaning towards the Data Science track. In this field, I would collect, analyse, and interpret data to find useful patterns, make predictions, and help organizations make better decisions.

Examples of Data Science applications include analysing large datasets, creating graphs and models, predicting trends, and using machine learning to solve problems.

Data Science appeals to me because I am fascinated by how much can be done with the right interpretation of data. I want to learn how to use data to discover patterns, make useful predictions, and support better decision-making. I am also interested in machine learning and the application of AI.

In four years, I see myself working in the field of Data Science, possibly at a technology company or another business or government agency. I would like to work on interesting, technical problems such as working on AI, detecting fraud, working on defensive technologies such as counter-drone systems, or using data to make useful predictions. By then, I hope to have learned more about programming, statistics, machine learning, and working with clients. I want to have gained experience working on real projects and understand how data can be turned into useful solutions. I'm not sure exactly which function or company I will choose yet, but I want to keep my options open.

Key skills for this field are a good foundation in mathematics and statistics, programming, problem-solving, critical thinking, and communication.
