Why IT?

Why?

  • Because I always want to keep learning new things, keep developing myself and never stagnate.

How?

  • By continuing to do what I already do: learn and apply course content, do an internship and get my degree.

What?

  • I am leaning towards applied data science & AI. I don't have a preference yet regarding the exact direction of work in the field.

My main reason for choosing to study HBO-ICT, besides my general interest in the field, is that I enjoy learning new things. IT is already a very broad field, even within its different specializations, but it is also a field that will continue to change and develop. This means there will always be something new for me to learn. I enjoy keeping myself busy with things such as learning new skills, solving complex problems, and creating solutions for them. I always want to keep learning and expanding my knowledge. Having something challenging to work on and continuously developing myself are two of the things that motivate me most in life.

I'm going to achieve my goal by continuing to learn new things at the HZ, applying them and continuing to work on my problem-solving capabilities. I am leaning towards the track applied data science & AI, but I don't really have a preference in exactly what direction I want to be working in the field. As long as it's interesting, there's something to do, like a problem to solve and I have the room to keep learning new things and keep developing myself, I don't really have a preference.
