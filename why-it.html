Why IT?

After I went to the HZ Open Day, I really liked both ICT and Finance & Control. ICT wasn't even originally my first choice. I was initially planning to only go to the Finance & Control open day, but my dad suggested that I also take a look at ICT because I might find it interesting. I did!

In the end I chose ICT over Finance & Control because the way of working suits me more. With Finance & Control you work with people the majority of the time. With ICT you also work with people on projects for example, but I think there's a big difference in the way people work together.

My dad used to work in the IT sector, my brother still does. I don't know a lot about the field, just that it's very broad, with a lot of different opportunties. I do know that the way they work right now is very different than it used to be. My brother complains sometimes that he misses just programming mindlessly, instead of managing agents.

My main motivation is just general interest. I think it's such a versatile course and when you learn it you can do so many different things with it. I expect to at least learn how to create and understand software. As far as achievements go, I don't really have anything too much in the future in mind, I hope to pass all my classes and make a fun game for the children in school.
